The base paper and my starting rule

Stage 1

Pre-selection

Choose the most important node as a core and grow a seed community around it, gated by a tightness test (CTS)

Stage 2

Evolution

Nodes relabel to the community that attracts them most (attractiveness A(c,i)), until labels settle

Stage 3

Label filtering

A weighted affiliation score (WAS) lets a node keep more than one label, which gives overlapping communities

TPOC-LPA was published in Expert Systems with Applications (2026). It competes with NINP-LPA and MFLP, both of which I also studied.

My rule from the start: reproduce the paper faithfully and match its numbers first; change nothing until that is done.

The base paper is TPOC-LPA, a three-stage label propagation algorithm that outputs overlapping communities. Stage 1 seeds communities from important nodes. Stage 2 lets nodes move to the community that attracts them most. Stage 3 decides which nodes belong to several communities. I chose it because it is the most recent of the four papers I read and it produces overlapping output. The rule I set at the start, and kept: reproduce first, change second.
