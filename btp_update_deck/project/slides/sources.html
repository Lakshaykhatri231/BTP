BTP progress update · Label propagation for community detection

7

Being clear about what is borrowed and what is mine. The motif idea comes from three published label-propagation papers that weight edges by triangles. The size-corrected update is the LPAm algorithm of Barber and Clark; the merge pass follows LPAm+ by Liu and Murata. Two things have no published source: the best-iterate guard and the granularity rule are my own designs, and I say so. I also want to be upfront that this is not a new algorithm family. LPAm, LPAm+ and LPA-MNI already exist. What I add is showing that the base paper's Stage 2 is the zero-correction case of a known family and unbounded, a way to choose between the regimes, and a map of where each works and fails.
