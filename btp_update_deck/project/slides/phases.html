Four phases. Phase 1: I implemented the paper and verified each formula against its worked examples. Phase 2: I tried adding a language model as a boundary judge, using three local models on a Kaggle GPU. It did not beat the graph structure, so I closed it as a negative result. Phase 3: I diagnosed the algorithm and found two things: Stage 2 can collapse, and the standard LFR generator in networkx does not produce the noise level it claims (nominal 0.6 gives about 0.84 realized), which I corrected by calibration. Phase 4: I added optional fixes and tested them carefully.
