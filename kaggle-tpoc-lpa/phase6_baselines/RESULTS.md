Every number marked *run* was computed by us on the same graph with the same metric code. Numbers marked *paper* are copied from Kong et al. (2026) for baselines without public code (NSA, SimCMR, WLC, UMSTMO, GREESE, MDPA) and are NOT re-run. `Infomap*` is not in the paper (extra baseline). `TPOC-LPA (repro)` = our reimplementation of the paper's algorithm; `Ours` = the best pipeline (`sim=tri_overlap, guard, refine=granularity, merge=mapeq, moves=synwalk`). NMI = sklearn arithmetic NMI; Q = unweighted modularity.
